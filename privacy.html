Before the store launches

Before launch: confirm the business responsible for personal information, a working privacy contact, hosting and GHL providers, payment services, retention periods, tracking tools and applicable privacy requirements.

01What this notice covers

This draft describes the local Ynotdecants website preview and its current features. It is not a complete notice for a future live GHL store. Live checkout, hosting, email, analytics and customer support services must be reviewed and included before launch.

02Your bag and favourites

The preview uses your browser’s local storage to remember shopping bag items and saved product identifiers. The storage keys are ynot-bag and ynot-saved. They are stored on your device rather than submitted to Ynotdecants by the preview code. They remain until you remove them or clear the website’s browser data.

03Forms and fragrance quiz

The contact form lets you enter your name, email address, enquiry topic, optional order reference and message. In this preview, submitting it only displays feedback in your browser: it does not transmit or save the message. The newsletter form likewise does not transmit or store your email. Quiz choices are used within the page to generate recommendations; the supplied quiz code does not submit those choices to a server. Saving a recommended fragrance uses the favourites storage described above.

04Website requests and providers

Pages load Google Fonts from Google’s font services. Your browser therefore makes requests to those providers, which may receive connection information such as your IP address. Image and script files are loaded from the website’s hosting environment. Hosting providers may keep technical access or security logs. Their actual practices and retention periods must be confirmed for the deployed site.

05Cookies and tracking

No analytics or advertising tracking scripts are configured in the supplied local preview code. This does not establish what a hosting platform, embedded tool or separately hosted GHL page may collect. Those services, cookies and consent controls must be checked before a live privacy notice is finalised.

06Your choices

You can remove the bag and favourites by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing storage will remove those saved preferences. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into the preview. When live services are connected, the notice will explain how to ask about access, correction, deletion, consent and other rights that apply to you.

07Security and retention

Do not enter passwords, card details or sensitive documents into the contact form. Local preview storage is not a secure place for sensitive information. Server-side security measures, processors, retention rules and international transfers need to be documented for the live service; no unverified security or retention promises are made here.

08Privacy questions

A working privacy contact and the responsible business’s identity will be published before personal information is collected through live forms or checkout. The Contact us page is currently a preview and cannot deliver privacy requests. This page will be revised to reflect the live service before launch.
