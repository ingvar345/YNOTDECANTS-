## Proposed interpretation, pending reference verification
- Mood: intimate, confident, sophisticated and premium.
- Photography: realistic perfume bottles, close-up glass and liquid details, dramatic shadows, warm highlights, restrained sensual editorial imagery with adult models where appropriate.
- Typography: consider an elegant editorial serif for headings and a clear sans serif for descriptions, navigation and prices. Exact families remain open.
- Layout: generous space, strong product photography, restrained decoration and clear shopping controls.
- Motion: subtle fades and gentle hover transitions with reduced-motion support.
- Maintain readable contrast and clear product names, sizes, prices and calls to action.
