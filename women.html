What notes are in the women’s collection?+

Explore each fragrance's notes, main accords and wear schedule in the product details. Use the brand filter or search to find a starting point.

Which decant size should I choose?+

Start with 2ml for a scent you have not worn before, 5ml to try it over more occasions, or 10ml for a favourite. How long it lasts depends on your atomizer and how much you spray.

Can I wear scents from any collection?+

Of course. Collections are a starting point. Follow the notes you enjoy, and browse the men’s, women’s and unisex edits whenever you feel curious.
