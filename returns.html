Before the store launches

Before live orders: confirm the return request window, eligibility for opened and unopened fragrances, return address, authorisation process, shipping costs and refund timing.

01Current store status

This storefront is a preview and has not accepted orders or payments through its local checkout. There is currently no live return or refund service connected to it. The final return conditions will be published before ordering becomes available.

02Starting a return enquiry

For a future order issue, provide the order reference, product name, size, a description of the issue and relevant photographs where helpful. Do not send a parcel to an unconfirmed address. A working support channel and return instructions will be provided before launch; the current contact form does not deliver messages.

03Return eligibility

A standard change-of-mind return period has not yet been set. Conditions for unopened, opened or used fragrance, exchanges and cancellations also need to be confirmed. This draft does not impose a blanket “no refunds” or “all sales final” rule, and does not limit rights that apply to faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods.

04Damaged, leaking or incorrect items

Keep the atomizer and packaging and note the problem. Photographs of the parcel, label and affected product may help support understand what happened. Do not use a product that appears unsafe or damaged. The appropriate remedy will depend on the circumstances and applicable rights.

05Sending fragrance back

Return shipping costs and responsibility for them will be stated in the final policy. Fragrance may require specific packaging and carrier services. Follow the confirmed return instructions and the carrier’s requirements rather than using an unsupported postal service.

06Refunds, exchanges and cancellations

The final policy will explain how a request is reviewed, how approved refunds are issued, expected processing times and whether exchanges or order changes are available. No refund deadline or deduction has been assumed in this draft. These arrangements must be confirmed before live payments are taken.

07Need more information?

Read the Shipping Policy for delivery information and Terms of Service for the store’s current status. The Contact us page will provide a support route once message delivery has been connected.
