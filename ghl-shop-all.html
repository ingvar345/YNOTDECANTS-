A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Have questions?

What is a perfume decant?+

A decant is fragrance transferred from its original bottle into a smaller, separate spray atomizer. It lets you try a scent before choosing a full bottle.

Which size should I choose?+

Start with 2ml for a first impression, 5ml to try it over more occasions, or 10ml to keep a favourite close. Choose 30ml for a fragrance you already know and love.

Can I explore any collection?+

Of course. Collections are a starting point. Follow the notes you enjoy and explore men’s, women’s and unisex fragrances whenever you feel curious.
