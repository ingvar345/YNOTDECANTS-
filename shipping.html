Before the store launches

Before live orders: confirm delivery countries, dispatch location, carriers, shipping rates, dispatch estimates, delivery estimates, tracking and any customs charges.

01Shipping is not live yet

The current website is a store preview and does not accept orders, payments or delivery bookings. No shipping service, free-shipping threshold or delivery date is offered through this preview. Final shipping information will be available before checkout opens.

02Delivery destinations and charges

Delivery countries, address restrictions and carrier options are still being confirmed. Final rates and any applicable order thresholds will be displayed before payment. Do not assume a destination is supported because it appears in your browser or in a sample image.

03Dispatch and delivery times

Dispatch is the time needed to prepare an order; delivery is the carrier’s transit time after handover. Both estimates need to be confirmed for the live store. No business-day turnaround or guaranteed arrival date has been set in this draft. Any live delivery promise must be shown clearly before purchase.

04Tracking and delivery questions

Available tracking options will be confirmed with the carrier services selected for launch. Once ordering opens, include your order reference and delivery concern when contacting support. A tracking status alone does not determine your consumer rights or the outcome of a delivery issue.

05Incorrect addresses, delays or damage

Check delivery details carefully before placing a future order. For a delayed, missing or damaged parcel, contact support with the order reference and relevant details. Keep the packaging and any photographs that may help investigate damage. The final policy will explain the support process without excluding rights that apply to faulty goods or delivery problems.

06Fragrance transport and international orders

Perfume can be subject to carrier and transport restrictions. Supported services, international delivery and any customs or import charges must be confirmed before orders are accepted. A return shipment should also follow the carrier’s requirements; see our Returns Policy.
