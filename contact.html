A little guidance.

Before you get in touch.

Can you help me choose a fragrance?+

Start with our fragrance quiz, or tell us which perfumes and notes you already enjoy. Mention whether you’re looking for something fresh, warm, floral or woody, and when you’d like to wear it.

What should I include in an order enquiry?+

Include your order number, the email address used for the order and a short description of your question. Please don’t include card numbers, passwords or other payment details.

Which decant size should I start with?+

Try 2ml for a first impression, 5ml to spend a little more time with a scent, or 10ml and 30ml for a favourite. How long a bottle lasts depends on the atomizer and how much you spray.
