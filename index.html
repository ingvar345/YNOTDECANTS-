Have questions?

Your decant questions, answered.

What exactly is a perfume decant?+

A decant is a smaller portion of a fragrance transferred from its original bottle into a separate atomizer. It lets you try a scent without buying a full bottle. The atomizer is separate packaging, rather than the manufacturer's original bottle.

Which size should I start with?+

Choose 2ml to get to know a new scent, 5ml to wear it more often, or 10ml for a fragrance you already love. How long a decant lasts depends on the atomizer and how much you spray.

Can I shop outside the men's and women's collections?+

Absolutely. Categories are a starting point. Wear whichever notes and fragrances you enjoy, and explore the unisex collection for more possibilities.

How should I store my fragrances?+

Keep your atomizers upright, tightly closed, and away from heat and direct sunlight. A cool, dry drawer is a good place to keep them between wears.

Can I place an order on this website?+

This is a store preview with catalogue products and estimated USD prices. You can explore fragrances, take the quiz, save favourites and try the shopping bag. Payment, fulfilment and live ordering will be connected before launch.
